## Mastery standard

Do not treat lesson completion as mastery. For each major stage, require the learner to:
- explain the concepts without notes;
- calculate representative values by hand;
- build or simulate the circuit;
- measure the real circuit;
- diagnose at least one intentionally introduced fault;
- read the relevant datasheet;
- document assumptions and limitations;
- complete a project that combines several lessons.

The existing courses already emphasize datasheets, measurement, non-blocking architecture, protocol reliability, hardware-resource conflicts, and systematic debugging. The expansion makes those ideas explicit across the whole learning path.
